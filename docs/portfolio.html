The pipeline

End-to-end: from raw ClinVar ingestion to a distilled on-device classifier. The core idea: only train on teacher reasoning that the evidence agrees with.

1,000 ClinVar germline variants (10-gene panel) + gnomAD allele frequencies, quality-filtered by review status

STEP 2

Evaluate
98-example validation split + 45 never-distilled holdout variants, scored against ClinVar labels

Headline results

Both students beat every comparator on both splits. The gradient-boosting baseline is a strong classical ML model trained on the same features the students see; the rule baseline encodes InterVar-style AF/LoF heuristics.

Validation (n=98) True holdout (n=45, never distilled)

The holdout set is the honest number: the validation split was also used for checkpoint selection. DeepSeek-R1 teacher is n/a on holdout (those variants were never distilled, so no teacher labels exist).

Key findings

Data quality beat compute

The jump from 56% to ~90% accuracy came from verifier-guided label refinement (keeping only teacher traces that agree with ClinVar), leakage removal, and dedup - not from bigger adapters or longer training.

Students exceed their teacher by ~17–20 points

The DeepSeek-R1 teacher scores 69.39% on validation, below even the rule baseline. The students only ever trained on correct reasoning, so distillation acted as a filter, not just compression.

Early stopping mattered

Validation loss bottomed at iteration 200 of a 1000-iter schedule. An automated watchdog stopped training; shipping the final checkpoint would have meant an overfit adapter (train loss 0.19, val loss 0.40).

Zero missed pathogenic variants

Pathogenic recall is 23/23 on validation and 7/7 on holdout for the 8B. The strongest classical baseline misses 4 of 23. Residual errors concentrate on the VUS ↔ Likely Benign boundary, where evidence separation is weakest.

The dataset

715 training / 98 validation examples after refinement, from 1,000 quality-filtered ClinVar variants (BRCA1/BRCA2-heavy panel plus MLH1). Prompts are verified leak-free: no labels, no label-conditioned hints.

Rare classes (Benign, Likely Pathogenic) were oversampled from 5/14 originals to 40 each. A separate 45-variant holdout was never distilled and is not shown here.

Pre-registered acceptance criteria

The evaluation protocol was fixed before the final training runs.

  • Beats the InterVar-style rule baseline8B 89.80% and 4B 86.73% vs 75.51% (valid); both 88.89% vs 73.33% (holdout)
  • Beats classical ML baselinesGradient boosting 81.63% valid / 73.33% holdout; logistic regression 77.55% / 71.11% - students win by +5.1 to +8.2 pts valid, +15.6 pts holdout
  • High-confidence precision ≥ 85%8B: 88.89% valid / 94.74% holdout; 4B: 85.48% valid / 100.00% holdout
  • Closes ≥ 40% of the teacher gap - metric degenerateThe teacher scored below the baseline, so the pre-registered gap does not exist; both students beat baseline and teacher outright instead
  • Reasoning integrity ≥ 70% on manual reviewPending: a 50-case blind community review bundle is prepared and circulating

Honest caveats

  • Narrow domain: ~99% BRCA1/BRCA2 (plus 10 MLH1 variants). Not evaluated on other genes.
  • Checkpoint selection used the validation split, so validation accuracy is mildly optimistic; the 45-variant holdout is the unbiased estimate.
  • Ground-truth labels are weak: 97% of variants are 1-star single-submitter ClinVar entries.
  • Small test sets (98 + 45) and tiny Benign / Likely Pathogenic supports make some per-class metrics unstable.
  • Research artifact. Not a medical device, not for clinical use.
